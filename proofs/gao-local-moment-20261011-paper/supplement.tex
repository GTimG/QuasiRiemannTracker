\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the full source-level arguments for the local
moment coefficient $2249/500$ and proposed zero-free boundary
$4374433/5000000$. It accompanies the
\href{paper.pdf}{main paper} and can be read independently. Appendix A
records the family, notation, exact certificate, and reproduction
instructions. Appendices B--I retain the complete signed reconstruction,
fixed dictionary, density, transport, local-moment, and endpoint arguments.
The proof is under review and depends on the pinned incumbent framework.
Exact arithmetic and selected compiled lemmas support it; independent
verification of the complete endpoint has not been completed.
\end{abstract}
